Fermion Research retrained the encoder's linear weights to five values and
quantized the remaining parameters to 6-bit integer tables. The encoder,
decoder, tokenizer and feature-extractor configuration derive from NVIDIA.

The app downloads the phonon-2-c repackaging by eschmidbauer:
https://huggingface.co/eschmidbauer/phonon-2-c/tree/1c91a6a7a26809fecedb35d847bef69663dde8bb
The five-value weights retain 2-bit sign and 1-bit magnitude planes.
Integer tables are widened to int8 with fp32 scales; fp16 tensors are widened
to fp32. Depthwise convolution BatchNorm is folded in double precision and
stored as fp32. The repackaging does not retrain or requantize the weights.

The Android integration adds JNI, CPU feature dispatch, and separate builds
for baseline ARM64 and dot-product CPUs. The upstream C source is unchanged.
Python conversion tools are not shipped in the APK.
