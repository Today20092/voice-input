Parakeet Redux Android integration

Model: Moondream Parakeet Redux, derived from NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B V3.
Weights: CC BY 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
https://huggingface.co/moondream/parakeet-redux
The app downloads converted packed GGUF weights by mudler/parakeet.cpp.
https://huggingface.co/mudler/parakeet-cpp-gguf
The conversion changes the storage representation; this app does not train
or fine-tune the model. The original weights are not bundled in the APK.
