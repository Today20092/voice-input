# App-wide diagnostics

Accepted design: collect lightweight standard diagnostics locally by default for all recognition models and the recording, model loading, live/final recognition, cleanup, delivery, and download stages. An off switch stops new standard records; Clear removes retained diagnostic history. Detailed mode adds stage/resource samples for 30 minutes, with a countdown and Stop action. Retain at most seven days and 10 MB, deleting oldest evidence first.

The user reviews the export contents and may enter what happened and expected behavior before manually sharing one ZIP. Include a readable summary, versioned structured records, device/build information, an allowlisted settings snapshot, and existing content-free S1-mini runs/benchmarks. Keep transcript-inclusive exports separate. Never automatically collect audio, dictated text, personal vocabulary, clipboard or surrounding text, receiving-app names, raw Logcat, URLs, or exception messages into standard diagnostics. User-entered report notes are explicitly included as entered.

Crash evidence is best effort. Managed stack frames and Android process-exit reason codes may help; native dumps and ANR trace streams are excluded. An unfinished session is evidence of interruption, not proof of a crash or hang. Delivery means the input connection accepted the commit or the recognition activity dispatched its result, not proof of how a receiving app rendered it.

Verification focuses on public collection and archive boundaries: opt-out, retention and size bounds, detailed expiry, corrupted-record recovery, content exclusions, and readable/structured ZIP contents. Android device checks must cover each recognizer, canceled and failed recordings, cleanup process failure, delivery failure, downloads, and sharing.

The standard-history budget reserves 7 MB for app events, 1 MB for S1 reports, and 2 MB for prepared app report ZIPs. Prepared ZIPs expire with their oldest included evidence and are pruned on launch/export; at most three are retained within the shared ZIP budget. If a ZIP would exceed that budget, oldest app events are omitted and counted in its summary. Expiry is enforced opportunistically on launch, collection, and export. Saved recordings and separately enabled transcript captures have their own existing retention policies.

The export contains `README.txt`, `summary.txt`, `environment.json`, `settings.json`, `events.jsonl`, optional `notes.txt`, and available `s1/runs.jsonl` and `s1/benchmark.json`. Event names and numeric metrics are versioned in `DiagnosticRecord`. Duration/elapsed values are milliseconds, memory fields specify bytes or KB, and exit reason/status values use Android's ApplicationExitInfo codes. Unknown S1 failure strings become `unclassified` rather than leaking arbitrary text.

History retranscription emits ordinary model-load, recognition, and cleanup timings under a session marked `RETRANSCRIPTION=1`. It ends with `RETRANSCRIPTION_FINISHED` only after saving the replacement transcript; cancellation and failure remain distinct. No recording ID, audio, or transcript text is added to the report. History uses full-clip recognition and must be analyzed separately from live dictation. Detailed mode adds resource samples at history stage boundaries, not continuous or guaranteed peak measurements.

`WAVEFORM_FIRST_FRAME` records the first waveform draw containing audio bars, including bars representing silence. Compare its elapsed time with `FIRST_AUDIO` and `RECORDER_STARTED` to investigate startup delays. It does not prove that the waveform stayed visible or moved. The settings snapshot includes `unobtrusiveRecognizer` so reports can identify the popup setting at export. The environment includes `gitRevision` to distinguish test builds sharing a version number. See the [phone test protocol](phone-performance-test.md) for passages, export instructions, and measurement limits.

Verified on 2026-09-25 with JDK 17 and Android SDK 35: `:app:testDevDebugUnitTest :app:lintDevDebug` succeeded. The suite reported 112 tests, zero failures/errors, and one skipped test; all ten new diagnostics tests passed. Standards and spec reviews found archive expiry and S1 failure-category omissions, both corrected and rechecked. No Android device was connected, so recording, visual/accessibility behavior, and share-sheet handling still require a device smoke test before release.
