## What the screenshot measures

Handy’s catalog supplies the exact title and the description “Tiny English model, virtually instant even on CPU.” The English benchmark reports 6.11% word error rate (WER) on the FLEURS test split with Q8_0 quantization. Handy reports 76.5× real time on the Ryzen 4750U’s Vulkan GPU and 50.0× on the 4750U CPU. Dividing 30 seconds by those rates yields 0.392 seconds on GPU and 0.60 seconds on CPU. These conversions describe benchmark throughput. Voice Input’s stop-to-text time also depends on model loading, preprocessing, postprocessing and delivery. The screenshot’s B+ is a Handy display grade, with no established relationship to an Android performance class. [Handy catalog](https://github.com/cjpais/Handy/blob/main/src-tauri/src/catalog/catalog.json) · [Handy benchmark](https://models.handy.computer/)

The catalog pins the Q8_0 GGUF at 135,373,280 bytes (129.1 MiB, 135.4 decimal MB). This build runs in transcribe.cpp and uses greedy TDT decoding. The conversion drops the auxiliary CTC head. Handy reports 2.43% WER on LibriSpeech test-clean for the same quantization, which illustrates how much the result depends on the test set. Neither throughput figure gives Android latency or memory. [Handy conversion card](https://huggingface.co/handy-computer/parakeet-tdt_ctc-110m-gguf) · [Pinned Q8_0 file](https://huggingface.co/handy-computer/parakeet-tdt_ctc-110m-gguf/blob/9d66d34f9e1594075c5dd72c90c0f4c321b29f21/parakeet-tdt_ctc-110m-Q8_0.gguf)

## Checkpoint, exports and runtime

NVIDIA and Suno’s `nvidia/parakeet-tdt_ctc-110m` is a roughly 114M-parameter hybrid FastConformer trained on about 36,000 hours of English speech. It accepts 16 kHz mono audio, emits cased and punctuated English, uses full attention and defaults to TDT in NeMo. Its greedy transducer WER ranges from 2.40% on LibriSpeech test-clean to 15.88% on AMI in NVIDIA’s table. The original download is a 459 MB NeMo checkpoint. [NVIDIA model card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt_ctc-110m) · [NVIDIA checkpoint files](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt_ctc-110m/tree/main)

## What it would add

The plausible niche is final-only English at roughly 132–136 MB installed, smaller than Phonon 2 (255.4 MB), Redux (213.3 MB), Orukeet (671.6 MB) and Parakeet TDT V3 (670.5 MB). Redux, Orukeet and V3 also cover 25 European languages. Phonon 2 and Whisper English are the most direct final-only English comparisons. The 43.6 MB Whisper English-39 already covers a smaller footprint. [Model guide](../model-guide.md)

ASR4ALL Small (38.6 MB) and included Medium (72.5 MB of APK assets plus a recoverable cache) already provide live English with built-in formatting. On an S25 Ultra, the same 11-second fixture took 523 ms with Small and 920 ms with Medium for whole-file decoding, with sampled app/test PSS of 273 and 324 MiB respectively. Those measurements give the prototype a local baseline. Moonshine Small and Nemotron English address live transcription. Handy’s FLEURS run offers a desktop comparison with some of these model families, with different artifacts and runtime. [ASR4ALL device report](ticket-26-asr4all-feasibility-2026-10-09.md) · [Model guide](../model-guide.md) · [Handy benchmark](https://models.handy.computer/)

## Proposed admission gate

1. **Verify artifacts and lifecycle.** Pin archive and payload sizes and SHA-256 digests. Smoke-test both heads on the shipping arm64 Sherpa AAR, then test download interruption, corruption, low space, deletion and rollback to an installed model. Record peak installation space, installed bytes and license notices. Confirm the signed build passes the catalog packaging check and works in airplane mode. [Model catalog release verification](../release/model-catalog-verification.md)
2. **Compare matched English recordings.** Use at least 200 held-out dictation clips with clean speech, multiple accents, background noise, names, numbers and short commands, plus a 2–5 minute recording to expose truncation and memory growth. Use identical audio, segmentation and normalization for raw recognition WER. Disable optional cleanup and vocabulary substitutions for that pass, then inspect delivered punctuation, casing, number formatting, insertions, repeats and dropped endings separately. Compare both heads with ASR4ALL Small and Medium, Phonon 2, Whisper English-39 and at least one larger final-only Parakeet option. Report paired confidence intervals and results by condition.
3. **Measure on two physical phones.** Use a recent S24/S25-class device and a meaningfully slower supported arm64 phone. Repeat paired cold and warm runs with a fixed, recorded thread count, power state and build. Report p50/p95 load time, decode real-time factor, stop-to-delivered-text at 5, 15 and 30 seconds, sampled peak app PSS and sustained slowdown over ten minutes of repeated dictation. Exercise IME, recognition activity, audio-history retranscription and cancel/retry, with zero crashes, OOMs or lost endings on the suite. [Model catalog release verification](../release/model-catalog-verification.md)
4. **Require a visible benefit.** Proposed thresholds for this experiment are at most 150 decimal MB installed, p95 warm stop-to-text within 1 s for 15 s dictation on the recent phone and 2 s on the slower phone, and peak app PSS within 500 MiB on both. Require aggregate WER within 1 absolute point of ASR4ALL Medium and no more than 3 points worse on any predefined accent/noise slice. Require either at least 1 absolute point better WER than ASR4ALL Small or at least 25% lower p95 warm stop-to-text than the fastest shipped final-only English comparator that meets the same accuracy limits. Prefer the simpler integration when both heads meet the gate with equivalent user-visible results.

The official TDT archive was downloaded and its SHA-256 and all four recognition files were verified. It is 108,035,095 bytes with SHA-256 `f628312e9fdf8686374cb01a69425c41732529d540860311f16f37cbc32cfe9b`. The extracted recognition files are pinned in the [app manifest](../../app/src/main/java/org/futo/voiceinput/parakeet/Parakeet110mModel.kt). Sherpa-ONNX 1.13.4 on Windows decoded the app's English speech fixture using those files, the NeMo transducer, and 80-bin features.

Phone tests, matched WER and formatting scores, and production AAR inference are pending. The thresholds above are proposals for comparative testing. The beta model's Android performance class is unmeasured.
