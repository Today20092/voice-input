## Parakeet 110M

The pinned candidate documents English-only offline recognition at 16 kHz mono, without translation or streaming. Conversion drops the auxiliary CTC head and uses greedy TDT/RNNT decoding; a future adapter must advertise final-only behavior. NVIDIA's original hybrid checkpoint offers TDT and CTC, but that does not give the converted GGUF a CTC decoder. [Candidate model documentation](https://github.com/handy-computer/transcribe.cpp/blob/4807edaf210d0d7e8a6f7fb2a44b65966a2797f0/docs/models/parakeet-tdt_ctc-110m.md), [pinned NVIDIA card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt_ctc-110m/blob/431a349/README.md).

An additional provenance gap exists in always-compiled Unicode helpers. `transcribe-unicode.cpp` explicitly ports llama.cpp MIT code; `transcribe-unicode-data.cpp` says its tables were copied verbatim from llama.cpp and generated from the Unicode Character Database. The candidate's notices omit separate llama.cpp/Unicode provenance and do not pin the donor revision or UCD version. Current llama.cpp's MIT copyright notice matches the vendored GGML notice, but that is not a verification of the historical donor. Unicode's current License V3 requires its copyright/permission notice with data/software or accompanying documentation. [Copied helpers](https://github.com/handy-computer/transcribe.cpp/blob/4807edaf210d0d7e8a6f7fb2a44b65966a2797f0/src/transcribe-unicode.cpp), [copied tables](https://github.com/handy-computer/transcribe.cpp/blob/4807edaf210d0d7e8a6f7fb2a44b65966a2797f0/src/transcribe-unicode-data.cpp), [current llama.cpp license](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/LICENSE), [Unicode license](https://www.unicode.org/license.txt).

For the reported CPU configuration with KleidiAI, OpenMP, system BLAS, GPU and dynamic backend loading disabled, the minimum conservative notice-copy approach is to retain the root MIT license, bundled third-party notices, authoritative GGML/miniz licenses, complete Mozilla tinyBLAS header notice if compiled, full Unicode notice and the copied-code provenance comments. Preserve model attribution separately if the model is distributed. A scan of the first 45 lines of the candidate's non-KleidiAI GGML CPU C/C++ headers/sources found no additional copyright/license declaration beyond tinyBLAS; separate whole-file spot checks of ARM quantization/repacking found none. This bounded scan is not a full provenance audit. Exact Unicode/donor provenance and final object/package contents remain checks before claiming complete distribution notices.

Model notices remain separate. Both pinned model cards identify CC-BY-4.0; redistribution needs attribution, the license link and identification of modifications/conversion. Preserve NVIDIA and converter provenance. [CC-BY-4.0 terms](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), [converted card](https://huggingface.co/handy-computer/parakeet-tdt_ctc-110m-gguf/blob/766f172fe70eb66785e3371664f53762e0fbafaa/README.md). ## Decision and remaining evidence

Source-level go for the explicitly pinned public Parakeet experiment; no-go for adopting FUTO's exact ASR4ALL integration from this candidate. Neither optional model is ready for production promotion. Actual JNI inference, cancel/unload behavior, same-process coexistence with legacy GGML and S1, complete packaged notices and measured phone benefit remain separate gates. A successful ARM64 link or isolated ELF inspection cannot pass those gates. Preserve Orukeet and saved selections while they remain unresolved.
