# 1.4.11-beta.4 · Spanish ASR4ALL and microphone settings

- **ASR4ALL Medium Spanish:** open **Model Options → ASR4ALL** to download and select it. The download is about 60 MB. It does not write the opening `¿` or `¡`, and it writes numbers as words. [Model source and license](https://huggingface.co/futo-org/asr4all-m-es)
- **Prefer Bluetooth** (off by default): starts recording on a connected Bluetooth microphone and remembers when you switch between Bluetooth and the phone while recording.
- **Pause videos/music** (on by default): the app already paused other audio; you can now turn that off.
- **Manual language selection** is now on by default. If you never changed it, Whisper with two or more languages asks which language you will speak. Saved choices are kept.
- Very short or empty recordings no longer reach the native Whisper code, and blank transcripts skip S1 cleanup.
- Credits now list NVIDIA's speech models.

The included English model and your existing selections are unchanged.

## Please test

The Spanish model ran on a desktop runtime but has not run on Android, and its accuracy has not been measured. Microphone routing and the pause setting have not been checked on a phone. Try Spanish dictation, Bluetooth with a headset (watch for lost opening words or an early stop), and dictation over music with the pause setting on and off.

Install this prerelease over the production-signed app to keep existing data.
